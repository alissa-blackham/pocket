Why can't it play whole songs from search?

Streaming services only let web pages play 30-second previews. For a full song, use an audio file you own. Pocket has to play the song itself (rather than listen to Spotify) because that's how it knows the song position to the millisecond. Pocket can't tell a down strum from an up strum by sound, so it only checks when you strum.
