# Pocket

A guitar timing coach that runs in your phone's browser. Pick a song, enter your strumming pattern and chords, and Pocket plays the song, counts you in and tells you as you play whether you're rushing or dragging.

- Search for a song (30-second Apple Music previews) or use your own audio file
- Follows the band's real beat, even when the tempo drifts
- Metronome locked to the song, with count-in
- Hears your strums through the mic, with the phone speaker or headphones
- Timing light: blue = perfect, green = good, amber = off, red = restart

Open it over https (it needs the microphone). Everything runs on the phone; nothing is uploaded.
